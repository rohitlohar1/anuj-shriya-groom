## ✨ Features & Architecture

- **Royal Opening Ceremony**: Embossed velvet doors with gold wax seal ("A & S - Shubh Vivah") and tap-to-unveil curtain animation.
- **Web Audio Classical Synthesizer**: Zero-dependency offline procedural audio engine creating Raag Bhupali Santoor plucks, Bansuri overtones, and continuous Tanpura drone.
- **Sacred Family Patrika / Heritage**: Dedicated lineage section displaying groom (Kothari family) & bride sides with full ancestral names and modal viewer for the official groom invitation card.
- **Interactive Scratch Card Reveal**: Realistic canvas scratcher to unveil wedding dates (23 & 24 Jan 2027) with gold foil shimmer and celebratory confetti shower.
- **Auspicious Countdown Clock**: Real-time ticker counting down to the celebrations.
- **Cherished Moments Carousel**: Responsive touch-swipe photo gallery of the couple with double-tap heart shower reactions and full-screen lightbox modal.
- **Interactive 2-Day Timeline**: Filter between All Events, Day 1, and Day 2 with expandable ceremony details.
- **Interactive Map & Directions**: Embedded live Google Map, direct navigation links, and one-tap address copying.
- **Phool Vrushti**: Interactive flower petal shower button with live blessing counter and pleasant chime audio.
